What this tool cannot know

  • It does not know what tenants actually pay. Most public rents are asking rents, so we discount them by an assumed amount.
  • The lease-speed assumptions (base rate and price sensitivity) are guesses. Public data on price versus days-to-lease for houses is thin. The sliders show how much they matter.
  • One house and one moment in time. There is no backtest, so we cannot say how accurate it is.
  • It does not know the house's condition, the landlord's finances, or this week's demand.
  • Market reports mostly describe apartments, not houses, so they are not used for the rent level.
  • It supports a decision; it does not make it.
