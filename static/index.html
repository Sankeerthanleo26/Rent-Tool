What this tool cannot know

  • What tenants actually pay. Most public rents are asking rents, so they are discounted by an assumed amount.
  • How fast houses lease at each price. The lease-speed assumptions are guesses; public data on this is thin.
  • One house at one moment, with no backtest, so accuracy cannot be measured.
  • The house's condition, the applicant pool, or whether another landlord changes price tomorrow.
  • Market reports mostly describe apartments, so they are not used for the rent level.

Rent Plan gives a decision estimate, not a guarantee. It supports a decision; it does not make it.
